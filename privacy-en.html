Privacy policy

Information under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for people who use Ubi (ubipin.it) and for people who receive an email notification from Ubi. This is an English translation; in case of doubt the Italian version prevails.

Data controller

Andrea Pernarcic — email: info@ubipin.it.
Write to this address for any request about your data.

What data we process

  • Account: email, password (stored only in encrypted form, unreadable by anyone), chosen name, date and version of the accepted privacy policy.
  • Content: photos, videos, audio, texts or links you upload, with title, hint, coordinates of the chosen place, unlock radius, any content password (stored encrypted) and the recipients' emails you enter.
  • Phone location: used only while the app is open, to pin content or to check that you are near the spot. It is not saved in your profile; for each unlock we record the date and the distance from the spot.
  • Public profile (optional): photo, bio and the contacts you choose to show (website, Instagram, WhatsApp, email), visible to users who can see your content.
  • Contacts: names and emails of the contacts you add to share content; visible only to you.
  • Messages: the private messages you exchange with other users, visible only to sender and recipient.
  • Email notifications: for each item, the list of addresses already notified and the date sent; the list of addresses that asked not to receive further notifications.
  • Replies to pins: texts, photos, videos or audio you leave for the creator of a pin you unlocked; only the creator and you can see them.
  • Ubi Dating (optional, adults only): date of birth (used only to check that you are an adult, never shown), gender (shown as a pink or blue dot on your notes), the optional tags you choose (e.g. orientation, what you are looking for, relationship style: visible only to Ubi Dating members), date of consents, dating notes and replies, «I want to meet you» choices, up to 6 dating profile photos (visible only to you and to people who gave you a double yes). In dating notes and replies your name stays hidden until you both choose each other.
  • Treasure hunts: if you complete a hunt, the prize code, the date of the win and of collection and your chosen name, visible to the hunt organiser (e.g. the venue) so they can give you the prize.
  • Contact groups and time capsules: the group names you give your contacts (visible only to you) and the opening date of your pins.
  • Sponsor banners: these are simple images with a link. We use no cookies or advertising tracking and we do not tell sponsors who sees or taps them: if you tap a banner you open the sponsor's website, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser data processed by the servers that run the site, access and security logs (e.g. wrong password attempts) to prevent abuse.

If you received a notification from Ubi without being registered

Your email address was given to us by a Ubi user who chose you as the recipient of some content. We use it only to notify you, once per item, and to give you access to that item if you sign up. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone. With the «I no longer want notifications from Ubi» link in every email you will receive no further notifications: we keep your address on an exclusion list precisely to respect this choice. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to info@ubipin.it.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service you requested: account, content, unlocking, profile, contacts, messages (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Use of the phone's location: your consent (Art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time from your phone or browser settings.
  • Email notifications to recipients chosen by a user: legitimate interest of the user and the controller in delivering the content (Art. 6.1.f), with the right to object at any time.
  • Security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).
  • Ubi Dating: taking part in a dating service may involve special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR); we process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9.2.a and 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time with «Leave Ubi Dating».

Who sees the content

You decide who sees each item: everyone, only you, or the emails you enter. If you enter someone else's email, make sure they agree to receive it. Do not publish content showing other people without their permission.

Providers processing data on our behalf

We do not sell or share data. To run Ubi we use these providers, which act as data processors or receive only the technical data they need:

  • Supabase (database, accounts, files): data stored on servers in the European Union.
  • Brevo (France): sending notification emails.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): hosting the site; receives the IP address of visitors.
  • OpenStreetMap, unpkg and jsDelivr: maps and site libraries; they receive the browser's IP address when it downloads maps and files.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (United Kingdom): address search; receives the text you search for and the IP address, only when you press «Search».

Some of these providers are based outside the EU: in these cases the transfer is based on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework, United Kingdom) or on standard contractual clauses.

How long

  • Account, profile, contacts and messages: as long as you keep your account.
  • Content: until you delete it. When you delete an item, its file and the log of related notifications are deleted.
  • List of people who no longer want notifications: until you ask to be removed, so we can respect your choice.
  • Security logs: for the time strictly necessary to prevent abuse.

To delete your account write to info@ubipin.it from the address you registered with: we delete all your data within 30 days.

Your rights

You can at any time request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent, by writing to info@ubipin.it. We reply within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority of your country.

Cookies and browser storage

Ubi does not use profiling, advertising or statistics cookies and does not track you on other sites. It stores in your browser only the technical data needed to work: the login session (to keep you logged in) and a few app preferences, such as the language. For this reason no consent is required (Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante's cookie guidelines, 2021). You can delete them at any time from your browser settings or by logging out; without them you will need to log in again. The services providing maps and libraries (OpenStreetMap, unpkg, jsDelivr) receive only the IP address needed to download the files.

Minimum age

The service is reserved for people aged at least 14 (the age of digital consent in Italy). The Ubi Dating section is for adults only (18+).

Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will ask you again in the app before you continue using it.
